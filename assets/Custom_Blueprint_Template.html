03 — Mindset

The Rules
of the Game

The artists who blow up aren't the most talented. They're the ones who play by different rules.

Rule 01
Volume beats perfection.
Every video is a lottery ticket. Post even when you're not sure — the algorithm has different taste than you. The more tickets, the faster things pop.
Rule 02
Instagram Trials is your most powerful lever.
Activate Trials on every Reel at publication. Minimum 3 days before publishing to your audience. Shows your content to non-followers first — for free. Most artists never use this.
Rule 03
When a video blows up — reuse its audio for 7 days.
The algorithm has found your audience. It keeps pushing content to people who already reacted to that sound. Ride the wave immediately.
Rule 04
Shares are the most powerful signal.
A share means someone connected enough to send it to another person. End CTA should always push for a share — "send this to a friend that loves {{GENRE_LOWER}}."
Rule 05
Never delete an underperforming video.
Deleting resets the algorithm's understanding of your page. Leave everything up — a slow video can get picked up weeks later.
Rule 06
Reply to comments in the first hour.
The algorithm reads activity as a quality signal. A burst of comment activity in the first 60 minutes tells the platform this content is worth pushing further.

Hashtag Strategy

Max 5 hashtags. 1–2 niche (#melodictechno), 1–2 community (#electronicmusic), 1 vibe (#summer2026). More than 5 signals spam to the algorithm.

Reading Your Curves

Video
Retention Curves

Your Instagram analytics show how people watch your videos. These 4 curves tell you exactly what to fix — and what to keep doing.

Good Video ✓
Gradual drop, stays above 50%. The algorithm reads this as quality content and keeps pushing it. More reach, more discovery.
Bad Video ✗
Drops to zero in the first 3 seconds. The hook isn't working. Fix: cut the opening, start at the best moment, or change the visual entry point.
Bad Momentum ✗
Steady decline throughout. The video starts okay but loses the viewer mid-way. Fix: shorten the video, cut dead time, or add a second hook at the halfway point.
Bad Explanation ✗
Cliff drop at a specific point. Something in the middle kills it — a caption that disappears, a transition, a slow section. Pinpoint exactly where the drop happens and cut that moment.
