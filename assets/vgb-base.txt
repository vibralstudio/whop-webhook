The rules of the game

Volume beats perfection. Post as much as possible.

Every video is a lottery ticket. The more you have out there, the faster things pop.

Post even if you're not sure.

The algorithm doesn't have the same taste as you. It can genuinely surprise you -- don't filter before posting.

Always use Instagram Trials Reels.

Minimum 3 days in Trials before publishing to the public. Extract maximum organic reach first. It's the most powerful lever on
Instagram.

As soon as a video blows up -- reuse its audio for ALL the ones after.

The algorithm keeps pushing your content to the audience that already reacted.

Never delete a video that underperforms.

Deleting a video resets the algorithm on your page. Leave everything up, even the flops.

Reply to comments within an hour of every post.

The algorithm reads activity as a quality signal and pushes the content further.

BRANDING -- SECTION BONUS
Understanding who you are as an artist

Viral content brings views. Branding creates real fans -- people who come to your shows, buy your merch, and want to be part of

your community. That's the difference between a view spike and a career.

Why branding is non-negotiable
The biggest artists have a recognizable visual universe, a unique tone, and an identity that makes people see themselves in

them. That's what creates loyalty -- fans who connect with who you are, not just your track.

- What colors, textures and visuals represent your universe?

- What feeling do you want people to associate with your name?

- What sets you apart from other artists in your genre?

- What type of fan do you want to attract -- and what unites them?

How to define your branding
1. Study the biggest artists in your genre. Observe their marketing, visuals, content patterns. Deconstruct what creates

attachment and loyalty in their fans.

2. Identify what is unique to you. Your story, your journey, your personality, your cultural references. Authentic branding starts

there -- not from a trend.

3. Use AI to do a full retrospective. Describe who you are, your music, your references, your values, your target audience. Let

AI help you articulate your universe and identify angles you wouldn't have seen on your own.

4. Build your branding bible. Colors, fonts, mood board, communication tone, priority content types. A reference document that

guides every creative decision.

The content that performs best is always the one aligned with who you truly are.

Fans who connect with who you are -- not just your music -- are the ones who stay, come back, and bring others.
